129 / Punched possibilities

Three offset warm punch cards overlap at unequal angles; a diagonally interrupted slot matrix creates a quiet blank path through the upper card.

composition

Broad physical card stack runs diagonally across the center, leaving wide bare margins; heading is printed on the top card itself.

silhouette

Three clipped-corner rectangles with differently angled long shadows and dense narrow punched slots.

typography

One bold 54px Vela is printed into the top card heading, with fine machine-like line text opposite.

material

Warm cardstock, punched dark apertures, angled edge shadows and registration rules.
