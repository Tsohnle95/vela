65 / Chromatic crossing

Scarlet, teal, ochre and violet ink planes collide across an interrupted black Orbit heading.

composition

Flat full-bleed print with four crossing diagonals; word cuts from top-left to center-right through a white seam.

silhouette

Huge black sans letters are separated across the diagonal; two small printer notes at lower-left.

material

Translucent spot-color shapes, tiny ink pinholes and offset edge registrations.
